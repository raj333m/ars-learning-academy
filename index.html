FAQ

Who is this for?

Business Analysts with around 10–15 years' experience targeting senior roles in regulatory reporting and data across the BFSI domain, including global capability centres and consulting practices.

Is the regulatory content UK-specific?

Yes. Credit risk reporting under the PRA Rulebook and UK CRR, Basel 3.1 as it applies in the UK from 1 January 2027, COREP and FINREP credit templates, and IFRS 9.

How are my answers rated?

Subjective answers are rated Excellent, Good, Satisfactory or Needs improvement with a score out of 10. Anything below Excellent comes with the correct explanation, key concepts and a worked example. Objective answers are marked right or wrong with an explanation.

What happens when I finish all 100 questions?

You get a fresh round: 50 new SQL and 50 new credit risk questions, avoiding everything you've already seen.

Is the AI Generated Certificate a professional qualification?

No. It's an AI Generated Certificate for practice, showing you passed this programme's timed exam.

How is my data used?

Your details and results are stored for this programme and visible to its admin for progress tracking and audit.

Interview Exam

Interview Exam: eligibility and instructions

Eligibility

  • You are registered, and your email () is correct. Your AI Generated Certificate is sent there.
  • You can give 60 uninterrupted minutes on a stable internet connection. A laptop or desktop is recommended.
  • The exam is for your own preparation: answer on your own, without notes or other help.

Instructions

  • 60 objective questions: Section A, 30 SQL; Section B, 30 UK credit risk regulatory reporting. Each has one correct option.
  • 60 minutes. The timer starts when you click Start exam and keeps running if you refresh or leave the page. When it reaches 00:00 your answers are submitted automatically.
  • One mark per question, no negative marking. Unanswered questions score zero, so answer every question.
  • Pass mark: 80%, which is 48 or more correct answers.
  • Move between questions freely, change answers, and mark questions for review using the question palette.
  • You see your result straight away with an explanation for every question.
  • If you pass, your AI Generated Certificate is emailed to you with your statistics (total questions, correct and incorrect answers, percentage). You can also download it.
  • Unlimited attempts, whether you pass or not; each attempt draws a fresh set of questions.
